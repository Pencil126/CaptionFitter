Speech models recognize speech on your Mac; your video is never uploaded. Larger models are more accurate but take longer to download and run. You need at least one model to align subtitles.

Open Settings (⌘,) and select the **Speech Models** tab. In the main window, you can also click the gear button next to **Model**, or **Download a Speech Model…** if you don't have a model yet.

## Download a model

The list shows your **Downloaded** models above the models you can download (**Available**, or **Other Available Models** once you have one). Each model shows its size, and some have a badge:

- **Best for This Mac**: the default choice for your Mac
- **Recommended**: suits your Mac

Click **Download**. A progress bar shows how far along it is; click **Cancel** to stop. A stopped download is marked **Download Incomplete**; click **Resume Download** to continue it.

Getting the list and downloading need an internet connection. If the list can't be loaded, check your connection and click **Refresh**.

## Choose a model

If you have more than one model, choose the one to use in either place:

- In the main window, under **Recognition**, from the **Model** menu
- In the **Downloaded** section, by clicking the circle next to a model

The app remembers your choice.

## Delete a model

Click **Delete** next to a model and confirm. The dialog shows how much space this frees. You can download the model again later. If you delete the model in use, another downloaded model is used.

## Storage

The bottom of the tab shows how much space the models use and the folder they're stored in. Click **Show in Finder** to open that folder.
