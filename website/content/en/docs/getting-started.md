CaptionFitter aligns a transcript you already have to the voice in a video and turns it into subtitles. It works for spoken-word videos and songs. Everything is processed on your Mac; your video is never uploaded.

## System requirements

- macOS 26 or later
- A Mac with Apple Silicon

## Install

1. Open the DMG you downloaded.
2. Drag **CaptionFitter** into the **Applications** folder.
3. Open CaptionFitter from the Applications folder.

The interface is in English or Traditional Chinese and follows your Mac's language; any other language shows English. To pick a language for this app only, go to System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications.

## Download a speech model first

CaptionFitter needs a speech model to align subtitles. Before you align for the first time:

1. Open Settings (⌘,).
2. Select the **Speech Models** tab.
3. Click **Download** next to a model. Models marked **Best for This Mac** or **Recommended** suit your Mac.

Until a model is downloaded, the main window shows a **Download a Speech Model…** button that opens the same tab. Downloading needs an internet connection. See the Speech models page for more.

## Make your first subtitles

The main window has the setup panel on the left, the preview at the top right, and the subtitle editor below the preview.

1. **Add a video or audio file.** Click **Choose a file, or drag one into the window**, drag a file into the window, or choose File › **Open Video or Audio…** (⌘O).
2. **Choose the language.** Under **Recognition**, set **Language** to the language actually spoken or sung in the video.
3. **Provide a transcript.** Under **Transcript**, choose **Paste**, **Transcribe**, or **Lyrics**. See the Transcript sources page.
4. **Align.** Click **Align Subtitles** (⌘↩). Progress appears at the bottom of the panel; click **Cancel** to stop.
5. **Review.** Check the marked lines in the subtitle editor. See the Review and edit page.
6. **Export.** Click **Export** in the toolbar (⌘E). See the Export page.

The first time you align after opening the app or switching models, the speech model has to load, so it takes a little longer.

If you click **Align Subtitles** before downloading a model, the app shows **No Speech Model Downloaded**; click **Manage Models** to go to the download list.
